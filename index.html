Advancing perinatal mental health through the lens of reproductive justice.

We are a network of 165+ psychologists, trainees, and allied professionals building a professional home for perinatal mental health within psychology, grounded in reproductive justice. We connect, share resources, and advocate for training that makes this work visible across the field.

Why This Network Exists

Mental health conditions are a leading cause of maternal death in the United States, and the burden falls hardest on Black, Indigenous, Latine, immigrant, and low-income birthing people. Yet psychology has no dedicated home for this work. Perinatal training is something psychologists piece together on their own, the research communities live largely in psychiatry, and reproductive justice, the framework naming the right to have children, to not have children, and to parent in safe and healthy communities, is rarely centered in either. We are building the room.

What We Do

Connection

Our member directory and listserv help psychologists and trainees find each other for collaboration on grants, manuscripts, symposia, mentorship, and referrals.

Resource Sharing

We surface training opportunities, research, and practical information our members need, including who is taking students and where training gaps exist.

Advocacy Through Training

We work to build perinatal mental health and reproductive justice competency into psychology training, from graduate coursework through specialty practice.

Get Involved

Whether you are a psychologist, trainee, student, or allied professional, you belong here. Joining takes two minutes and adds you to our member directory and listserv. Guided by a founding steering committee, the network is just getting started.
